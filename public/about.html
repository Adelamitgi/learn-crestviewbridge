About & credits

HSK 4 Study Desk is a free daily study tool for the new HSK 4 exam (HSK 3.0). It groups the Level 4 words by shared characters, schedules reviews with spaced repetition, and adds grammar drills, graded reading, writing and speaking practice, and study timers. Everything runs on your device.

Not official. This site is not affiliated with Chinese Testing International (CTI), the Center for Language Education and Cooperation (CLEC) or Mandarin Zone. Confirm exam dates, format and registration at chinesetest.cn.
